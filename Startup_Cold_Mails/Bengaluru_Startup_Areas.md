# Bengaluru IT Startup Hubs

Here are the top areas in Bengaluru that are known as major hubs for IT startups and tech companies:

1. **HSR Layout** (Currently known as the "Startup Capital" of Bengaluru, heavily packed with early-stage and mid-stage IT startups)
2. **Koramangala** (The original startup hub of the city, still very active with tech companies)
3. **Indiranagar** (Popular for both tech startups and design/product agencies)
4. **Outer Ring Road (ORR) / Bellandur** (Home to massive tech parks, MNCs, and large-scale IT startups)
5. **Whitefield** (A major IT hub, heavily populated with software companies and tech parks)
6. **Electronic City** (One of India's largest electronic and IT industrial parks)
7. **Marathahalli** (Surrounded by tech parks, highly populated with IT professionals)
8. **BTM Layout** (Popular for early-stage startups and affordable office spaces)

*Note: If we are going to collect emails for IT roles (like Software Engineer, AI/ML Engineer, Data Scientist), these are the areas we would target when searching for startup companies in Bengaluru.*
